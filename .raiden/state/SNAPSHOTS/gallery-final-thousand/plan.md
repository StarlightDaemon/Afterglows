blocks and one rigid bar with a pen beyond the first pivot. Let A=(26 cosθ,0),
B=(0,26 sinθ), P=(46.8 cosθ,-20.8 sinθ), relative to (70,65). AB=26 and AP=20.8
remain constant, and P traces an ellipse with semiaxes 46.8 and 20.8. A dim full
locus supplies context while a short luminous trail follows the pen. Eight-second
closed cycle; reduced motion freezes a diagonal pose with the complete locus.
This is a two-slot ellipsograph, distinct from the existing pantograph scaling
linkage and spirograph rolling-circle study.
